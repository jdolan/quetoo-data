Model description       : 


Magdalena is my second released Player model for quake3. The name is from an 'A Perfect Cirle' song, which I was playing when I first saved the model, and I needed a name. It got stuck to it. Magdalena is what came out of a project that Bobotheseal and I were going to do, he unfortunatly had to drop out in the design stages but he left me with the idea of making a character with big arms, that supported most of it. I was very pleased about the outcome of the model I modeled pretty fast.

The animations were the first set I ever did with Max4 bones, they're awesome to work with. After about 5 different setups I animated Magdalena, and here is the final result. The animations are a little excessive, and perhaps a bit high on framecounts. It runs pretty good in game, mainly because of the LODs. The Deaths will look a bit weird on opponents, because of the long length, but when you die as a player, they look as they should. I wish I had done some more with the upper body (the turret) so I could have made a better taunt and other anims for that. It didn't turn out too bad though.

The skin all turned out great thanks to the awfully talented skinning team. Dark Horizon made default, and pretty much made that skin about as I had it in mind, but alot better. The other skins are all awesome, and different, just as I hoped.
