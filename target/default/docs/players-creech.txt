Model description:       "Creech"

"Through the combination of genetic material borrowed from both alien 
and human sources, The Creech is an in vito engineered life form. He is the 
science of cloning's epitome of achievement. Extreme measures where taken 
when creating The Creech's mind. He was raised in complete isolation where 
only controlled data was fed into his brain. Such carefully chosen bits of 
information enable him to function on a much higher level of consciousness 
than normal. The result is a eing that, although lacking sentience, 
possesses a mind untainted by the concept of limitation, the absence of 
which in turn removes all physical limitations."

This Creech model was suggested by Arco, one of the skinners who worked with us 
on him. We considered doing Creech because of his interesting characteristics, 
one being his head is part of the torso and sticks out the center of his chest, 
accompanied with the flowing hair tendrils just added to the fun and would work
great with the hidden head technique. We noticed from the original pictures sent
to us by Arco that it looked like his boots might fire off like jet boots, so he
seemed like an all around fun project. ;) After trying and failing to get a PPMs
correctly into Team Arena we were forced to do the next best thing. A mini conversion
for Team Arena that would not be considered a conventional PPM. What we did was 
completely removed the James character and replaced him with Creech we also did 
over 500 custom sounds that are being used for the head models. We wanted Creech
to have a voice that sounded monster like but still be audible.  The Team Arena skins
That were done are different from the quake 3 skins there more colored for the game.
With this model in Team Arena no head models can be changed in game you will only 
notice the new head model in the HUD and the voice will change. 
This pk3 should work with pure server and team arena demo.

The animations:

The animations are all custom we used over 700 frames of animation on Creech.
This was not possible with the old Q3data.exe converter because it would crash at
Over 510 frames but we got our hands on a new converter that we have tested 
to over  850 frames so it looks like it is unlimited one thing to note is that when
doing an export of the ASE file you will have to put a zero for the last digit in the
qdt file to export correctly because it will ask for the first frame of head animation.  
This is still an unknown and we will be looking into it.
Hope you all can do some fun animations with out restrictions.
Example: Creech.qdt  file
$aseanimconvert models/players/Creech/Creech.ase   -playerparms 130 390 0 
you can get it here http://www.mindlessgrafx.com/Q3data-JukesBros.exe


Back ground story for "Monturo" skin by: James "HarlequiN" Taylor
We liked the story so we thought we would put in in. ;)

Specimen 218 looked up to the sky and saw the moon for the first time, framed silver in a halo of clouds.  
A momentary diversion, it seemed to last a lifetime.  The spell was broken by the crack of a twig behind 
him, and he was running once more...

The lab in which he'd spent most of his life was long behind him now, but the soldiers followed him still, 
he was a prize they could ill afford to lose.  Out of 220 genetic mixes, only 218 had lived to reach maturity.  
He had been well treated for a time, his wishes mostly granted, music in his quarters fulfilling his love of 
Metal music, clothes in the styles he chose tailored to fit his massive frame, though band T-shirts were out 
of the question, for obvious reasons.  When it reached 18 years of age though, things began to change.  The 
head of the genetics lab regrettably passed away leaving his position to a bureaucrat more interested in how 
much the specimen cost them than what it's needs might be.  It's quarters were locked and it was left to rot 
in it's own filth.

Time passed, and far from becoming tamed by it's incarceration 218 became determined to escape.  Months 
of hard work honed its muscles and reflexes, months of living in it's cell hardened it to disease.  Eventually 
it was ready to make its move.

The guard was taken by surprise as the seemingly lifeless creature rose up from its sickbed and grasped him 
by the throat - he did not have time to feel another emotion.  218 had of course been feigning its illness, and 
now it had  its chance.  The alarms did not rig until the Guards body was discovered 1twelve minutes 
later...

For another hour the creature ran, smashing through ranks of trees, wading through swamp.  The pursuers 
trailed him still.  "Enough" it thought - it's mind more acute than his jailers had thought, now it was time to 
stand and fight.  Turning to face the dogs that had reached him first he flexed his muscles and prepared for 
this attack.  No longer "it" - he was "he", no longer "specimen" - he was MONTURO.

James "HarlequiN" Taylor
