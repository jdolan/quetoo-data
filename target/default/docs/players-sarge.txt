
SUW Captain Otherian the 3rd, Exterminator of the Mons.

At 6 years old Otherian had is first encounter with the Mon, his family ripped from him, taken to a Mon offworld to 
be used as seen fit by Tark the Mon Overlord. He himself escaped by hiding under the dead, however he did not escape 
untouched. Some type of Mon Virus had infested his system, probably contracted through the dead in which he had 
hidden. This virus gave him the ability to know where the Mons are,a kind of 6th sense, Which he now uses to guide
him and his Men to where they are. In the hope that one day he will meet Tark and avenge his family. But time is 
Short and the virus is spreading quick, Only he know what he is becoming.

Quote

"If you see a Squid face the best thing to do is stick your rail in its face and pull the trigger."

" I will purify this universe, in the field or in the Arena. The Mon must be exterminated, No Man or Mon will
prevent me from this."
